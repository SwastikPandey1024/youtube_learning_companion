> [!note] Definition: **Vector** ([00:45](https://youtube.com/watch?v=DrCeIbpfuzE&t=45s))
> An ordered collection of real numbers in n-dimensional Euclidean space $R^n$.
> 
> **Notation:** $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$

> [!abstract] Theorem: **Linear Transformation Property** ([05:10](https://youtube.com/watch?v=DrCeIbpfuzE&t=310s))
> **Statement:** Linear transformations preserve vector addition and scalar multiplication.
> 
> **Conclusion:** Linear transformations preserve vector addition and scalar multiplication.

#### 📐 Formula: Linear System Matrix Equation ([07:00](https://youtube.com/watch?v=DrCeIbpfuzE&t=420s))

$$
A \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}
$$

**Variables:**
- `$A$`: coefficient matrix
- `$x$`: unknown vector
- `$b$`: target vector

> [!example] Worked Example ([09:00](https://youtube.com/watch?v=DrCeIbpfuzE&t=540s))
> **Problem:** Compute dot product measuring vector alignment in cosine similarity.
> 
> **Solution:**
> - Take vectors u and v
> - Sum products of corresponding elements
> 
> **Result:** u \cdot v

> [!caution] GATE Exam Trap (Direct from lecture) ([13:00](https://youtube.com/watch?v=DrCeIbpfuzE&t=780s))
> **Mistake / Pitfall:** Confusing linear independence with orthogonality.
> 
> **Correct Concept:** Orthogonal vectors are linearly independent if non-zero, but the converse is false.
> 
> **Why Tested in GATE:** Both describe geometric separation between vectors.
